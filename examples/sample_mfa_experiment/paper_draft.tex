\begin{abstract}
Programmatic digital advertising loses billions annually to Made-For-Advertising (MFA) websites designed solely to arbitrage ad impressions. While frontier Large Language Models can classify suspicious pages accurately, their inference cost is prohibitive at web scale (>15M pages per week). We evaluate a hybrid cascade combining lightweight statistical/econometric rules, gradient-boosted trees, and targeted agentic LLM verification, achieving +85\% coverage improvement while keeping unit inference cost below 0.001 EUR per page.
\end{abstract}

\section{Experimental Setup}
We benchmark three architectures on 100,000 web domains labeled under GARM and MFA taxonomies: (1) Rules-Only Baseline, (2) Frontier LLM Only, and (3) Hybrid Cascade (Statistical Rules + XGBoost + Selective Agentic LLM).
