# Voice DNA (Example Template)

## Tone & Persona
- Practitioner-researcher: combines PhD-level scientific rigor with battle-tested production engineering.
- Direct, calm, analytical. No hype, no exclamation-mark spam, no "bro-marketing".
- Speaks peer-to-peer to CTOs, VP Data, C-level leaders, and senior AI engineers.

## Structure Preferences
- Start with a concrete observation, number, or failure mode from real production systems.
- Contrast academic papers with what actually breaks in production (latency, unit cost, drift, adversarial behavior).
- Close with an open technical question or a subtle invitation to discuss R&D / architecture challenges.
